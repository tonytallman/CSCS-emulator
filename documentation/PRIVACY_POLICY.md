# Privacy Policy — CSC Emulator

Effective date: June 25, 2026

CSC Emulator ("the app") is developed and maintained by Tallman Software.

## Data we collect

The app collects no personal data. It does not use analytics, crash-reporting services, advertising networks, or any third-party SDK that transmits data off your device.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth Low Energy solely to advertise a simulated cycling sensor to nearby devices at your explicit request. No data is transmitted to any server or third party. Bluetooth activity occurs entirely on your local device and the devices you choose to connect.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party code or SDKs. No data is shared with any third party.
